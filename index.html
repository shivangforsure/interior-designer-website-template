About Us

Crafting Timeless Interiors Since 2015

Where Vision Meets Reality

Studio Haven Interiors is a boutique interior design firm dedicated to creating sophisticated, functional spaces that reflect the unique personality of each client. With over eight years of experience in residential and commercial design, we believe that exceptional interiors are born from a perfect balance of aesthetics, functionality, and soul.

Our founder, Elena Martinez, graduated from the prestigious Rhode Island School of Design and has been featured in Architectural Digest, Elle Decor, and Interior Design Magazine. Her approach combines timeless elegance with contemporary sensibilities, ensuring that every project stands the test of time.

We pride ourselves on our meticulous attention to detail, collaborative design process, and unwavering commitment to excellence. From initial concept to final installation, we guide our clients through every step, transforming their vision into spaces they'll love for years to come.
